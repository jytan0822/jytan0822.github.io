Welcome! My name is Jonathan Tan, and I am a PhD student at the
<a href="https://ieor.columbia.edu/">Department of Industrial Engineering and
Operations Research</a> at Columbia University. I am fortunate to be co-advised
by Prof. <a href="http://www.columbia.edu/~ae2516/">Adam Elmachtoub</a> and
Prof. <a href="https://ykanoria.github.io/">Yash Kanoria</a>. Prior to joining
Columbia, I recieved my bachelor's degree at the
<a href="https://www.math.pku.edu.cn/index.htm">School of Mathematical
Sciences, Peking University</a> in 2024.

My research focuses on pricing and market design. I am interested in designing and analyzing service systems enabled by modern AI technologies, and in applying queueing theory, mechanism design and optimization to develop efficient and socially responsible policies for these systems.
